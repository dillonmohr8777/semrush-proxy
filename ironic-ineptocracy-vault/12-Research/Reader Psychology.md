# Reader Psychology

> Why people read political fiction and what drives purchase decisions.

## Why People Read Political Thrillers
- To process anxiety about real-world power systems through fiction
- To feel intelligent - political fiction signals sophistication
- To find language for feelings they can't articulate about institutions
- Entertainment that doesn't feel like wasted time
- To validate their skepticism about systems

## Purchase Decision Factors
1. **Cover and title** - Does it look serious and intelligent?
2. **Description** - Does it promise depth without being pretentious?
3. **Reviews** - Do other smart readers vouch for it?
4. **Excerpt/sample** - Does the writing quality match the promise?
5. **Author credibility** - Is there a reason to trust this voice?
6. **Timeliness** - Does it feel relevant right now?
7. **Recommendation** - Did someone they trust recommend it?

## Emotional Triggers That Drive Action
- Recognition: "This book gets it"
- Curiosity: "I need to know how this plays out"
- Status: "This is the kind of book I want to be reading"
- Urgency: "This matters right now"
- Belonging: "Other thoughtful people are reading this"

## Implications for Marketing
- Lead with recognition and curiosity, not hype
- Social proof from the right people matters more than volume
- The title itself is a psychological hook - lean into it
- Excerpt quality is the strongest closer after interest is generated
