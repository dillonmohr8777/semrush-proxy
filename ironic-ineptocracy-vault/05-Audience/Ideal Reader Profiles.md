# Ideal Reader Profiles

> Who this book is for. Drives all targeting, content angles, and messaging.

## Profile 1: The Politically Aware Thriller Reader
- **Age range:** 28-55
- **Reads:** Political thrillers, conspiracy fiction, literary suspense
- **Watches:** Political dramas, documentaries about institutional power
- **Listens to:** News analysis podcasts, political commentary
- **Frustrated by:** Thrillers that are all plot, no substance
- **Entry point:** "Political thriller about corruption" searches
- **What hooks them:** The premise that the system is designed this way
- **Where they are:** Goodreads, Reddit r/books, political book lists

## Profile 2: The Literary Fiction Reader Who Wants Stakes
- **Age range:** 25-50
- **Reads:** Literary fiction with social commentary, contemporary realism
- **Watches:** Prestige TV, character-driven dramas
- **Frustrated by:** Literary fiction that's all style, no plot
- **Entry point:** Theme-based searches (surveillance, meritocracy, race in fiction)
- **What hooks them:** The thematic depth paired with thriller pacing
- **Where they are:** Substack, literary Twitter, independent bookstores

## Profile 3: The System-Skeptic
- **Age range:** 22-45
- **Reads:** Non-fiction about power, systems, institutional critique
- **Watches:** Documentaries, investigative journalism
- **Frustrated by:** Feeling like they see the problem but can't name it
- **Entry point:** "Books about institutional corruption" or "fiction about power systems"
- **What hooks them:** The title itself - "ineptocracy" names what they feel
- **Where they are:** Reddit, Twitter/X, podcast audiences, news comments

## Anti-Profiles (NOT Our Reader)
- People looking for light beach reads
- Readers who want clear heroes and villains
- People uncomfortable with political themes
- Readers who want everything resolved neatly
