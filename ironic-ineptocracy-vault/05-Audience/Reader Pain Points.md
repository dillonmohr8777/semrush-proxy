# Reader Pain Points

> What frustrations drive our ideal readers to seek out a book like this.

## Genre Frustrations
- "Political thrillers are all action, no depth"
- "Literary fiction is too slow and stakes-free"
- "Every thriller has the same corrupt-politician plot"
- "I want fiction that makes me think, not just entertains"
- "Books about power are either too simplistic or too academic"

## Real-World Frustrations (Turned into Reader Motivations)
- "I can see something is wrong with the system but can't articulate it"
- "The news feels like theater - I want fiction that goes deeper"
- "I'm tired of narratives that reduce complex problems to individual villains"
- "Nobody in fiction is writing about the actual machinery"
- "Every conversation about race and power gets oversimplified"
