# Objection Handling

> Common reasons someone might NOT buy, and how content addresses each.

## "Is this too political?"
- **Response:** The book explores systems, not parties. It asks questions, doesn't campaign.
- **Content:** Blog post - "Why The Ironic Ineptocracy Isn't a Political Book (Even Though It Is)"

## "I don't read thrillers"
- **Response:** This is a literary thriller. Character-driven, thematically deep, with thriller pacing.
- **Content:** Position on "literary fiction with stakes" angle

## "Never heard of this author"
- **Response:** Every author starts somewhere. The work speaks for itself.
- **Content:** Strong author page, excerpts, early reviews, social proof

## "Sounds heavy / depressing"
- **Response:** It's intense but not nihilistic. The characters have agency. The story moves.
- **Content:** Reader testimonials, excerpt that shows pacing and voice

## "I'll wait for reviews"
- **Response:** Give them a taste now. First chapter, quotes, theme content.
- **Content:** "Read the first chapter" page, quote cards, newsletter excerpts

## "What's an ineptocracy?"
- **Response:** This IS the hook. Explain it and they're already curious.
- **Content:** Title meaning page (SEO value + conversion tool)
