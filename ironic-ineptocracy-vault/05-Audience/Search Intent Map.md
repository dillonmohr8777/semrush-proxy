# Search Intent Map

> Mapping what people search to what they actually want. Drives SEO + content strategy.

## Informational Intent (Top of Funnel)
> Searching to learn or explore - not ready to buy

| Search Query Pattern | What They Want | Our Content |
|---------------------|---------------|-------------|
| "books about surveillance state" | Recommendations | List post featuring our book |
| "political thriller themes" | Understanding genre | Theme explainer + book positioning |
| "what is an ineptocracy" | Definition | Title meaning page |
| "novels about race and power" | Recommendations | Theme page |
| "fiction about institutional corruption" | Discovery | Blog post + book page |
| "meritocracy in fiction" | Analysis | Essay-style blog post |

## Navigational Intent (Mid-Funnel)
> Searching for something specific

| Search Query Pattern | What They Want | Our Content |
|---------------------|---------------|-------------|
| "The Ironic Ineptocracy" | The book itself | Homepage |
| "The Ironic Ineptocracy characters" | Character info | Character pages |
| "Dillon Mohr author" | Author info | About author page |
| "Ironic Ineptocracy review" | Social proof | Reviews / press page |

## Transactional Intent (Bottom of Funnel)
> Ready to buy or act

| Search Query Pattern | What They Want | Our Content |
|---------------------|---------------|-------------|
| "The Ironic Ineptocracy buy" | Purchase link | Pre-order / buy page |
| "The Ironic Ineptocracy kindle" | Format-specific | Buy page with format options |
| "political thriller new release" | Recent books | Blog / landing page |
