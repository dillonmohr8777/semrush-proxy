## Character Page Structure
1. **Character name** (H1)
2. **Role tagline** - One sentence about who they are (no spoilers)
3. **Description** - From approved public description in `02-Characters/`
4. **Key relationships** - Who they connect to (links to other character pages)
5. **Themes they embody** - Links to theme pages
6. **Key quote** - From Character Quote Bank (spoiler-safe)
7. **CTA** - "See how [character]'s story unfolds → Read the book"

## SEO Value
- Each character page is a rankable asset for "[character name] the ironic ineptocracy"
- Character pages create internal linking opportunities
- Long-tail traffic from character-specific searches
- Builds topical authority around the book's entity
