# Email Capture Strategy

> Building the pre-release list and ongoing reader base.

## Signup Points
| Location | Offer | Priority |
|----------|-------|----------|
| Homepage | "Get the first chapter free" | High |
| Blog post footer | "Join the reading list" | High |
| Dedicated landing page | "Be first to know + free chapter" | High |
| Character pages | "Get character dossiers" | Medium |
| Theme pages | "Deep dive into [theme]" | Medium |
| Exit intent popup | "Before you go - free chapter" | Medium |

## Email Sequence (Post-Signup)
1. **Immediate:** Welcome + first chapter PDF
2. **Day 3:** Author's note - why this book exists
3. **Day 7:** Character spotlight - meet Darnell
4. **Day 14:** Theme deep-dive - what the book is really about
5. **Day 21:** Social proof (reviews, press) or community invite
6. **Launch day:** "It's here" + buy links

## Ongoing Newsletter
- Frequency: Bi-weekly or monthly
- Content: Behind-the-scenes, theme essays, reading recs, updates
- Tone: Personal, insider, thoughtful (see `04-Brand/Voice and Tone.md`)
- CTA: Varies by content (read blog, share, buy, review)
