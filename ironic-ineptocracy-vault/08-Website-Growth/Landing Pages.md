## Landing Page Template
Each landing page should include:
1. Keyword-optimized H1
2. Compelling intro (match search intent)
3. Substantive content (800+ words)
4. Internal links to book page and related content
5. CTA appropriate to the page's funnel position
6. Schema markup where applicable
