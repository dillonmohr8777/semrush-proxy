## CRO Principles for This Site
- Intrigue > hype (match the book's tone)
- Value exchange for email (give something worth signing up for)
- Multiple conversion opportunities per page (don't rely on one CTA)
- Mobile-first (most book discovery happens on phones)
- Speed matters (slow pages kill conversions)
