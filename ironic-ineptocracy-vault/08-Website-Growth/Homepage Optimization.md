## Ideal Homepage Structure

### Above the Fold
1. **Hero headline** - Primary tagline (from `04-Brand/Taglines.md`)
2. **Sub-headline** - One sentence that expands the hook
3. **Primary CTA** - "Read the first chapter" or "Pre-order now"
4. **Book cover image** - High-quality, properly sized

### Below the Fold
5. **Book description** - 2-3 paragraphs from `01-Story-Core/Novel Summary.md`
6. **Theme badges** - Quick visual tags: Political Thriller | Surveillance | Power | Race
7. **Character preview** - Brief character cards linking to character pages
8. **Social proof** - Reviews, endorsements, press mentions (when available)
9. **Author section** - Photo + brief bio + link to about page
10. **Email signup** - "Get the first chapter free" with form
11. **Blog preview** - Latest 3 posts
12. **Secondary CTA** - Repeat primary CTA
