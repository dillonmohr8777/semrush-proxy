## Theme Page Structure
1. **Theme title** (H1, keyword-optimized)
2. **What this theme means in the novel** - 2-3 paragraphs
3. **Why it matters now** - Real-world relevance (careful: no partisan takes)
4. **How the novel explores it** - Without spoilers, reference character involvement
5. **Related reading** - Comp titles that explore similar themes
6. **Key quotes** - From the novel that touch this theme
7. **Related blog posts** - Internal links to cluster content
8. **CTA** - "Explore these themes in The Ironic Ineptocracy"

## Why Theme Pages Are Critical
- They rank for informational queries with high discovery potential
- They establish topical authority Google rewards
- They give organic visitors a reason to explore the site
- They connect naturally to blog content (cluster model)
- They differentiate from every other book site that only has a "buy" page
