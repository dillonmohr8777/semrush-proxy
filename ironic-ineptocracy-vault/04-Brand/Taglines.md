## Tagline Options (Testing)

### Theme-Forward
- "The system isn't broken. It was built this way."
- "What if incompetence is the design?"
- "The machine works perfectly. That's the problem."

### Intrigue-Forward
- "Some systems aren't meant to be fixed."
- "He saw the machinery. The machinery saw him first."
- "The irony isn't that it failed. The irony is that it worked."

### Reader-Forward
- "For readers who already suspect the truth."
- "A political thriller that assumes you're paying attention."
- "Fiction for people who read between the headlines."
