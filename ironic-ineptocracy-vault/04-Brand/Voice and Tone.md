# Voice and Tone

> How The Ironic Ineptocracy speaks in all marketing channels.

## Brand Voice Attributes
- **Intelligent** - assumes the reader is smart
- **Direct** - says what it means, doesn't hedge
- **Layered** - surface reads clean, deeper reads reward attention
- **Provocative** - asks uncomfortable questions
- **Confident** - doesn't apologize for what it is

## Tone by Channel

### Website
- Clean, authoritative, intriguing
- Short sentences. Let ideas land.
- No exclamation points. No hype language.

### Blog / Long-form
- Essayistic, thoughtful, not academic
- Can be longer and more exploratory
- Still direct - no throat-clearing paragraphs

### Social Media
- Sharp, quotable, visual
- One idea per post
- Questions > statements (engagement driver)
- Dark humor welcome when appropriate

### Email / Newsletter
- Personal, insider tone
- "Here's what I've been thinking about" energy
- Reward subscribers with depth, not promotions

### Press / Outreach
- Professional but not corporate
- Lead with the book's relevance, not self-promotion
- Frame around themes and timeliness

## Words We Use
- Machinery, system, structure, design
- Interrogate, explore, expose, reveal
- Power, control, awareness, cost
- Deliberate, constructed, engineered

## Words We Never Use
- Page-turner, must-read, gripping, riveting (generic thriller praise)
- Delve, tapestry, journey (AI tells)
- Game-changing, groundbreaking (self-aggrandizing)
- Simple, easy (the book isn't)
