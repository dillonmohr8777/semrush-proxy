# TikTok Strategy

## Account
- Handle: [TBD]

## Content Types
1. **Hook videos** - "If you like books about [theme], you need to read this"
2. **Theme explainers** - 30-60s on what the book explores
3. **BookTok responses** - Stitch/duet with "looking for recs" videos
4. **Character intros** - Brief, punchy character introductions
5. **Behind the scenes** - Writing process, author life

## Hook Templates
- "POV: You just read a political thriller where the system is the villain, not a person"
- "What if I told you incompetence in government was designed that way?"
- "A book that makes you question every institution you thought was broken"
- "Name a thriller where the twist isn't who did it, but that the system did"

## Posting Cadence
- 3-4 videos per week
- Best times: evening (7-9pm)

## Production Notes
- Keep it raw and authentic - overly produced BookTok underperforms
- Vertical video, good lighting, direct to camera
- Text overlays for hooks
- Trending sounds only when they genuinely fit
