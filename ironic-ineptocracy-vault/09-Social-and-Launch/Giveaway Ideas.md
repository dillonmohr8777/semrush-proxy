# Giveaway Ideas

> Strategic giveaways that build audience, not just give away books.

## Pre-Launch Giveaways
1. **ARC Giveaway** - Free early copies in exchange for honest reviews
2. **First Chapter + Signed Bookplate** - Email signup incentive
3. **"Name the System" Contest** - Social engagement: "What institution would you expose?" = follows + engagement

## Launch Giveaways
4. **Launch Week Bundle** - Signed copy + character dossiers + discussion guide
5. **Book Club Kit** - 5 copies for a book club (apply via email)
6. **Goodreads Giveaway** - Platform-native, builds Goodreads presence

## Platform-Specific
| Giveaway | Platform | Goal | Mechanic |
|----------|----------|------|----------|
| ARC copies | Email list | Reviews | Signup + request |
| Signed copy | Instagram | Followers | Follow + comment |
| Book club kit | Email | List growth | Apply via form |
| Digital copy | Goodreads | Ratings | Goodreads giveaway |

## Rules
- Every giveaway must grow an owned audience (email, follows)
- Never do "tag 5 friends" spam mechanics
- Prefer quality engagement over viral reach
