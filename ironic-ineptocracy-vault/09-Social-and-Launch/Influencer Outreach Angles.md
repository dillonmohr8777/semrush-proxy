# Influencer Outreach Angles

> How to approach BookTok, Bookstagram, and book community influencers.

## Target Profiles
- BookTok creators who review political fiction / dark thrillers
- Bookstagram accounts that feature literary thrillers
- Book reviewers on YouTube (BookTube)
- Substack writers covering fiction + politics
- Podcast hosts in the political fiction space

## Pitch Angles

### For Thriller Reviewers
- "A political thriller where the system is the villain, not a person"
- Emphasize: pacing, complexity, dark tone

### For Literary Reviewers
- "Literary fiction with real stakes - political thriller meets social commentary"
- Emphasize: thematic depth, character complexity, prose quality

### For Political/Social Commentary Creators
- "Fiction that goes deeper than the headlines on institutional power"
- Emphasize: meritocracy, surveillance, race, relevance

### For Debut Author Champions
- "A debut that doesn't read like a debut"
- Emphasize: fresh voice, unique concept, the title's hook

## Outreach Rules
- Personalize every message (reference their content specifically)
- Offer ARC, no strings attached
- Never promise or request positive reviews
- Follow up once, then move on
- See `10-Outreach/Outreach Templates.md` for message templates
