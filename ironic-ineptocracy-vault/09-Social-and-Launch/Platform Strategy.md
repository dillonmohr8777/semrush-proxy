# Platform Strategy

> Where to show up, how, and why.

## Platform Priority

| Platform | Priority | Primary Use | Audience Fit |
|----------|----------|-------------|--------------|
| X / Twitter | High | Quote cards, theme threads, engagement | System-skeptics, literary readers |
| Instagram | High | Visual quotes, behind-the-scenes, cover reveal | Broader book community |
| LinkedIn | Medium | Author credibility, thought leadership | Professional readers |
| TikTok | Medium | BookTok hooks, short-form theme takes | Younger readers, discovery |
| Reddit | Medium | Organic community participation | Deep readers, niche communities |
| Substack | Low-Medium | Long-form newsletter crossover | Literary, essayistic readers |

## Content by Platform

### X / Twitter
- Quote cards (1-2 daily)
- Theme questions ("What if the system isn't broken?")
- Thread: "5 things political thrillers get wrong about power"
- Engagement: Reply to book recs, political fiction discussions
- Tone: Sharp, concise, provocative

### Instagram
- Quote graphics (branded visual design)
- Behind-the-scenes (writing process, desk, inspiration)
- Character spotlights
- Cover reveal (when ready)
- Stories: polls, questions, countdowns
- Tone: Visual, atmospheric, mysterious

### LinkedIn
- Author essays on themes (meritocracy, institutions, power)
- "Why I wrote this" angle
- Professional credibility content
- Tone: Thoughtful, professional, relevant

### TikTok
- "Books where the system is the villain"
- "What is an ineptocracy?" explainer
- 30-second theme hooks
- Character introductions
- Tone: Direct, hook-driven, not cringe

### Reddit
- Genuine participation in r/books, r/suggestmeabook, r/booksuggestions
- Answer "looking for books about..." posts naturally
- Never self-promote transparently - add value first
- Tone: Authentic, helpful

## Posting Cadence
| Platform | Frequency | Best Times |
|----------|-----------|-----------|
| X / Twitter | Daily | Morning + evening |
| Instagram | 4-5x/week | Late morning, evening |
| LinkedIn | 2-3x/week | Weekday mornings |
| TikTok | 3-4x/week | Evening |
| Reddit | As natural | When relevant threads appear |
