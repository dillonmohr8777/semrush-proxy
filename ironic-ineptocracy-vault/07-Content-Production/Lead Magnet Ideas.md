# Lead Magnet Ideas

> Free assets that capture email addresses.

## Top Candidates

### 1. First Chapter PDF (Highest Priority)
- Simple, proven, directly sells the book
- Requires: excerpt permissions, formatted PDF, landing page
- CTA: "Read the first chapter free"

### 2. Discussion Guide
- Appeals to book clubs and group readers
- Requires: themed questions, character discussion prompts
- CTA: "Get the book club discussion guide"

### 3. Author's Note on the Themes
- Behind-the-scenes perspective on why the book exists
- Requires: essay from Dillon, designed PDF or email sequence
- CTA: "The story behind the story"

### 4. Character Dossiers
- Designed profiles of main characters (spoiler-safe)
- Requires: canon-approved descriptions, visual design
- CTA: "Meet the characters before you read"

### 5. Curated Reading List
- "10 Books That Shaped The Ironic Ineptocracy"
- Requires: comp research, designed list
- CTA: "See what inspired the novel"

## Evaluation
| Lead Magnet | Effort | Conversion Potential | Audience Fit |
|------------|--------|---------------------|--------------|
| First Chapter | Low | High | All profiles |
| Discussion Guide | Medium | Medium | Book clubs |
| Author's Note | Low | Medium | Literary readers |
| Character Dossiers | Medium | Medium | Thriller readers |
| Reading List | Low | Low-Medium | Discovery readers |

## Priority: Start with First Chapter, add others over time.
