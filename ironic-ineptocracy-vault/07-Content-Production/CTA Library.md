# CTA Library

> Pre-approved calls to action for different contexts. Keeps messaging consistent.

## Primary CTAs (Pre-Launch)

### Email Signup
- "Get the first chapter free"
- "Join the pre-release list"
- "Be the first to know when it drops"
- "Get early access and behind-the-scenes updates"

### Read More
- "Read the first chapter"
- "Meet the characters"
- "Explore the themes"
- "See why this book matters now"

## Primary CTAs (Post-Launch)

### Buy
- "Get your copy"
- "Read it now"
- "Available on [platforms]"

### Review
- "Leave a review if you've read it"
- "Tell a reader who needs this book"

## CTAs by Placement

### Blog Post End
- "If this resonated, The Ironic Ineptocracy goes deeper. [Read the first chapter →]"
- "Curious? [Get the first chapter free →]"

### Character Page
- "Want to see how [character]'s story unfolds? [Read it now →]"

### Theme Page
- "The Ironic Ineptocracy explores [theme] in ways fiction hasn't before. [Learn more →]"

### Social Bio
- "Author of The Ironic Ineptocracy. [Link]"

### Email
- "Hit reply and tell me what you think."
- "Forward this to someone who needs to read this book."

## CTA Rules
- Never use "Buy now!" energy
- Always match the CTA intensity to where the reader is in the funnel
- Blog readers → soft CTA (read more, get chapter)
- Theme/character page visitors → medium CTA (read first chapter, preorder)
- Returning visitors → direct CTA (buy, preorder)
