### H2: The Standard Political Thriller Formula

- Why it works and why it's getting stale

### H2: What's Missing: The System as Antagonist
- Most political thrillers blame individuals for systemic problems
- Real institutional failure is more complex than one bad actor
- The most interesting question isn't "who did it" but "who designed it this way"

### H2: The New Wave of Political Fiction
- Authors and books pushing the genre forward
- Cross-pollination with literary fiction
- International perspectives on political power
- Include The Ironic Ineptocracy as part of this wave

### H2: What Readers Actually Want
- Intelligence, not simplification
- Relevance, not escapism
- Characters who navigate systems, not just fight villains
- Fiction that asks questions instead of prescribing answers

- 5 books that represent the evolution of political thrillers
