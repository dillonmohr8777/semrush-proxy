# Public Spoiler Rules

> What can and cannot be revealed in public-facing marketing content.

## Safe to Reveal (Tier 1 - Open)
> Use freely in all marketing materials
- Genre and tone
- Character names and basic descriptions
- General premise (without specific plot mechanics)
- Core themes
- Setting basics
- The title's meaning (general)
- Author background

## Tease Only (Tier 2 - Hint)
> Can reference existence of, but not specifics
- The nature of the "machinery"
- That a manufactured conflict exists
- Character relationships (existence, not evolution)
- That the system is the antagonist
- General emotional stakes

## Never Reveal (Tier 3 - Protected)
> These are off-limits in all public content
- Plot twists
- Character deaths or fates
- The specific mechanism of the manufactured war
- How the collision plays out
- The ending
- Any "the real villain was..." reveal
- Specific betrayals or alliance shifts

## Rule
When in doubt, tease less. The book should be discovered through reading, not marketing.
