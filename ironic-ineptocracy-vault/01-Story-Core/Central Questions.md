# Central Questions

> The questions the novel asks. These drive content angles and reader engagement.

## The Big Questions

1. **What happens when meritocracy is a performance?**
   - 

2. **Who benefits when the system appears broken but is actually working as designed?**
   - 

3. **What does it cost to see the machinery?**
   - 

4. **Can individuals disrupt systems built to absorb disruption?**
   - 

5. **What is the relationship between surveillance, control, and the illusion of freedom?**
   - 

## How These Drive Content

Each question becomes a content cluster:
- Blog posts that explore the question through the lens of current events
- Theme pages that position the book as exploring these ideas
- Social posts that pose the question to spark discussion
- Interview talking points that frame the book's relevance

## Reader Entry Points

Different readers will enter through different questions:
- **Political readers** → Questions about institutional power and corruption
- **Literary readers** → Questions about identity, cost of awareness
- **Thriller readers** → Questions about surveillance, manufactured conflict
- **Social commentary readers** → Questions about meritocracy and race
