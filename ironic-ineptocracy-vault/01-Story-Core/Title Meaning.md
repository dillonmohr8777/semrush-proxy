# Title Meaning

> Why the book is called The Ironic Ineptocracy.

## Definition
- **Ineptocracy** - A system of government where the least capable to lead are elected by the least capable of producing, and where the members of society least likely to sustain themselves are rewarded with goods and services paid for by the confiscated wealth of a diminishing number of producers
- **Ironic** - The irony that this system isn't accidental. It's designed. The ineptocracy isn't a failure of governance - it's a feature.

## What the Title Signals
- This is a book about systems, not accidents
- The incompetence is the point
- The title itself is a thesis
- It signals intelligence - readers who pick this up know what they're getting into

## Marketing Implications
- The title is a conversation starter - use it
- "What does ineptocracy mean?" is a natural search query
- The irony angle creates content hooks: "The irony of..." framing
- Title explanation pages have SEO value
- The title differentiates from generic thriller titles

## Title in Copy
- Don't over-explain the title in marketing copy - let it intrigue
- Use the title as a lens: "In a world that rewards ineptitude..."
- The title should make people Google it - that's the point
