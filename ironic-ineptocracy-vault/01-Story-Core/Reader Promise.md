## What This Book Is
- A political thriller that doesn't insult your intelligence
- A novel where the system is the villain, not a single person
- Fiction that makes you look at real institutions differently
- A story about what power actually looks like when it stops pretending

## What This Book Is NOT
- A partisan political screed
- A dystopia set in an imagined future (this is recognizable now)
- A simple hero-vs-villain narrative
- Preachy or moralistic
- An easy, escapist read - this one stays with you

## Who This Is For
- Readers who want their fiction to make them think
- People frustrated with systems they can see but can't name
- Fans of political fiction that goes deeper than surface-level conspiracy
- Readers who appreciate morally complex characters
- Anyone who's looked at an institution and thought "this can't be accidental"
