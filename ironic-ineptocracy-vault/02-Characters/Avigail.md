# Avigail

> Canonical character profile. All public-facing content must align with this.

## Role
- Supporting character. Darnell's love interest. Represents hope, normalcy, and a parallel dream.

## Background
- Israeli, born and raised in downtown Jerusalem (7-minute walk from the Mamilla Hotel)
- Hotel clerk at the Mamilla Hotel
- Accepted to Harvard for Law before the war suspended her visa indefinitely
- Family: parents and four sisters. Father is a rabbi. Mother stays home.
- Never left Israel before joining the group

## Physical Description
- Short frame and stature - estimated 5'1" or shorter
- Piercing green eyes (a deliberate parallel to Alec's, but representing safety instead of danger)
- Petite but massive in personality

## Personality
- Kind and hospitable on the surface
- Prone to depressive episodes and anxiety (known to Leah, hidden from Darnell initially)
- Increasingly reserved and withdrawn as the journey progresses
- Deeply conflicted about leaving her family and country
- More perceptive than she lets on - internally critical of Darnell and Javon's materialism despite not voicing it
- Capable of sudden outbursts when pushed ("SHUT THE FUCK UP, WE KNOW. IS THAT YOUR WHOLE PERSONALITY?")

## Internal Conflict
- Did she come to America for Darnell, or for Harvard? She questions her own motivations.
- Guilt over leaving her family in a war zone
- Growing resentment at comments about material things (the Macan) when people are dying
- Wonders if she's using Darnell and the group as her ticket to America
- "I roped Leah into this too. Where the fuck is she going to go after this is done?"

## Her Deeper Perspective
Avigail sees America differently than the Americans do. She grew up in a country defined by war. She recognizes the architecture of oppression because she lived inside it. When she looks at Orange County, she doesn't see beauty - she sees "the embodiment of Capitalism" and wonders if America is any different from what she fled. Her internal monologue is the most politically sophisticated in the book despite never voicing it.

## Arc Summary
- **Start:** A hotel clerk who helps a wounded American soldier. Hopeful. Attracted to the idea of Harvard and Darnell.
- **Evolution:** Grows increasingly conflicted about her motivations. Becomes quieter as the journey continues. Questions whether Darnell and Javon truly understand systemic oppression or just perform it.
- **End:** Chooses to continue despite her doubts. "I have no answer and absolutely no idea. Maybe that's the fun of it."

## Key Relationships
- **Darnell:** First romantic relationship for both. Physical and emotional connection. But she privately questions whether it's love or strategy.
- **Leah:** Best friend since childhood. Leah is her anchor. She feels guilty for dragging her along.

## Approved Public Description
> Avigail was seven minutes from everything she ever knew. A hotel clerk in war-torn Jerusalem, she was supposed to be at Harvard studying law. Instead, she bandaged a wounded American soldier, fell for him, and made a decision that would take her across the world. She sees things the others don't - the architecture beneath the freedom, the cost behind the marble. She's not sure if she's chasing a dream or running from a nightmare. Maybe both.
