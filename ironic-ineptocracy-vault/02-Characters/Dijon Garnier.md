# Dijon Garnier

> Canonical character profile. All public-facing content must align with this.

## Role
- Primary antagonist. The true power behind the presidency and the war.

## Background
- French billionaire, son of French immigrants (baking empire)
- Net worth: $272.8 billion (Forbes). Wealthiest person on the planet.
- Holds nearly 2.5% of the U.S. GDP. More wealth than 41.2% of the entire U.S. population.
- Father of 12 children (largely neglected)
- Companies: Tecca (EVs/autonomous driving), BankIt (cashless payments), NeuroClick (brain chips), ConnectIt (election monitoring), aviation
- Lives in a $47.8 million Orange County, California mansion
- Not a U.S. citizen originally - French immigrant who became American

## Physical Description
- Not physically imposing
- Known for his cigars
- Lacks charisma and is awful at public speaking despite being magnetic in private
- "He was an angel investor, not the fucking engineer"

## Personality
- Master manipulator and marketer - can bend anyone's will
- Brilliant but not in the way people are - he's a strategist, not an inventor
- Secretly loathes McNulty despite appearing to be allies
- Increasingly maniacal over time - starts as a "titan of industry" and devolves into a genocidal autocrat
- Addicted to power, attention, and control
- Erratic - can't contain himself, reveals too much when angry
- Recently became "more religious minded" - "just in case"

## Rise to Power
1. **BankIt:** Founded America's first cashless payment system by destroying competitors through leaked fabricated stories, buying them at 60% discount, then integrating with every major U.S. bank. $1.3T valuation.
2. **Tecca:** Electric vehicles with first autonomous steering capability. 167 accidents, 119 deaths from the technology. Brutal union-busting led to 1.6 million total firings over a decade.
3. **NeuroClick:** Brain chip company. Publicly for paralysis treatment. Secretly for memory erasure and mass population control.
4. **ConnectIt:** Election monitoring technology. Secretly rigged ballot boxes for McNulty.
5. **Citizens United:** Played major role in the 2011 Supreme Court case, buying off five conservative judges.

## Motivations
- **Power over money.** He has more money than he can spend. He wants control.
- **Union suppression.** Black union leaders organizing in his companies made him "look weak." This is what triggers the entire plot.
- **Revenge against Black leadership.** Black men accounted for 17% of union leadership at Tecca. He blamed them for his declining net worth after a recession.
- **Attention.** Deep insecurity about lacking charisma. People respected his money and products but "didn't actually love the man."
- **Legacy.** Wants to be remembered, but on his terms.

## The Plan (Operation BI - "Black Intellects")
1. Bribe 70% of sitting U.S. Senators ($4.45 billion total)
2. Coordinate with Russia (President Ivanov) for false-flag attack on Israel using faux Iranian agents
3. Manufacture social media outrage through Russian deep fakes
4. Get Congress to declare war and reinstate the draft
5. Use CIA to identify the smartest young Black Americans (Ivy League, MIT, Stanford)
6. Draft them specifically and send them to die in the manufactured war
7. Deploy NeuroClick chips to erase the American public's memory of the war and McNulty's third term
8. Imprison families of draftees in internment camp in Sonoran Desert, Arizona

## Key Relationships
- **McNulty:** Uses him as a puppet. "I am the first and I am the last." Secretly loathes him. Infects McNulty with the chip himself.
- **Ivanov (Russia):** Alliance of convenience for the false-flag operation
- **Newman (CIA Director):** Initially had to coerce with death threats, then Newman became an eager participant
- **Alec Daheim:** His former soldier, now his enemy
