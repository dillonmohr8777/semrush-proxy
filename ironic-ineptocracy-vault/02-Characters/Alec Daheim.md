# Alec Daheim

> Canonical character profile. All public-facing content must align with this.

## Role
- Deuteragonist / Anti-hero. Former antagonist turned ally.

## Background
- Born into a military family
- Mom passed at age 4. Twin older brothers killed by IEDs in Iraq when Alec was 9.
- Father became abusive after losing both sons. Raised Alec to be a soldier.
- Bodylifting by age 12. Benching 300 by 17. 2360 SAT.
- Pilot's license at graduation (never really used)
- Military service in Iraq - came out traumatized
- Yale University - Global Affairs degree in 3 years, graduated before age 23
- Career path: House Rep protective aide (Pennsylvania) → Senator aide (Oklahoma) → CIA Protective Agent / Field Operative for Director Newman
- Salary: $500K annual (blackmail money - must never live above means)
- Secret second apartment in Tabb, VA - a 2,900 sq ft home he bought off the books

## Physical Description
- 6'5", 235 lbs
- Piercing green eyes - "his soul was somewhere in Iraq, while his brain was in top form"
- Frame suggests NFL quarterback, never played a sport
- Surgical scar on right side of head from NeuroClick chip implantation

## Personality
- Self-described sociopath (admits it openly)
- Drives recklessly (regularly 100+ mph)
- Bone-dry humor, often inappropriate
- Extremely competent at everything physical
- Multilingual (speaks Hebrew fluently, likely more)
- Deeply loyal to whoever earns his trust
- Capable of extreme violence without hesitation
- Under the surface: scared of death, craves normalcy, wants to be a professor

## Motivations
- Initially: Blind loyalty to the CIA chain of command
- After chip removal: Retribution against the people who erased his identity
- Secretly: Wants a normal life. "I could have been a Professor of Global Affairs."

## The Chip
- Memory erased by NeuroClick chip after being compromised (Darnell/Javon broke into his apartment)
- Became a Russian soldier puppet for Garnier's operation
- Chip broken by Javon's headbutt in the Spanish mountain facility
- Memories returned rapidly after chip dislodged
- Side effects: eczema-like skin reaction to sunlight

## Arc Summary
- **Start:** CIA agent following orders. Surveils Darnell, pistol-whips him in Philly. A machine.
- **Turning Point:** Javon headbutts him, breaking the chip. He wakes up as himself.
- **Evolution:** Becomes the group's tactical leader, using his CIA training to steal planes, hotwire cars, acquire weapons, and navigate hostile territory.
- **End:** Leading the assault on Garnier's mansion. Still a sociopath, but one pointed at the right target.

## Key Relationships
- **Sabrina ("Echo"):** Love interest and former CIA partner. He carried her through a blizzard in Belarus after she was shot. The only person who humanizes him.
- **Javon:** Saved his life. They have antagonistic banter but deep mutual respect.
- **Darnell:** The person he wronged most. Darnell never fully trusts him.
- **Director Newman:** Former boss who ordered his chipping. Now his enemy.
- **Mark:** Fellow rogue ex-CIA. The weapons connection.

## Defining Moments
1. Nearly killing Garnier with a red dot years before the main events
2. Pistol-whipping Darnell in Philadelphia
3. Being chipped and losing his identity
4. Javon headbutting him back to consciousness
5. Stealing a plane in Malaga
6. His vulnerability speech about wanting to be a professor
7. Methodically killing 14 guards at Garnier's mansion

## Approved Public Description
> Alec Daheim followed every order. Military service. Yale degree. CIA operative. He was the perfect soldier - loyal, lethal, and unquestioning. So when his superiors told him to surveil innocent civilians, he didn't ask why. When they implanted a chip in his brain to erase who he was, he couldn't fight back. But someone fought for him. Now Alec is awake, he's angry, and he has every skill the government gave him pointed back at the people who used him. The question is whether the man underneath the training is someone worth saving.
