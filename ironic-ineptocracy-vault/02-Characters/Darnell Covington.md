# Darnell Covington

> Canonical character profile. All public-facing content must align with this.

## Role
- Protagonist. Co-lead alongside Javon Whitfield.

## Background
- From West 7th Street, East Atlanta, Georgia
- African American, raised upper-middle class
- Attended Thomas Jefferson High, a predominantly white private school (0.5% Black population)
- Math prodigy - taking Calculus by age 13
- First African American (male or female) from his school to be accepted to an Ivy League school
- Perfect 1600 SAT, 36 ACT, 4.9 weighted GPA
- Harvard-bound for law
- Valedictorian of his graduating class

## Family
- **Brittany (Mom)** - Night nurse on the medical surgical unit, 6 blocks from home. Roman Catholic upbringing. Warm, supportive, worked late shifts.
- **Charles (Dad)** - City cop in East Atlanta. Strict. Worked 55+ hour weeks. Arrested friends' fathers and brothers. Complicated relationship with Darnell. 
- **Tana (Sister)** - 8 years old. Piercing blue eyes mixed with dark complexion. Sharp-tongued. "This is her castle."
- **Roxanne (Grandmother)** - Died the night before Darnell's SAT from a fall/hemorrhage. Married Francis, a white vacuum salesman. Close bond - Sunday visits for ice cream and golf/tennis watching. Her death is a defining loss.

## Physical Description
- 6'1", initially 170 lbs (gains ~20 lbs during military training to ~190)
- Growing afro (grew it out to spite white classmates)
- By the end: curly beard, hardened appearance

## Personality
- Shy, reserved, intensely focused
- Never the athlete - his exercise was walking 25 minutes to school
- Obsessively academic - grades and Harvard were his entire identity
- Naive about politics and history (his weakest subject, only class he got a B in)
- Non-confrontational... until the war changes him
- Never smoked, drank, or had a girlfriend before the events of the book

## Motivations
- Initially: Get into Harvard. Become a lawyer. Nothing else matters.
- After the draft: Survive. Find his family. Get home.
- By the end: Destroy the system that weaponized his intelligence against him

## Internal Conflict
- Control vs. chaos: Darnell has always been in control of his life. The draft strips that away.
- Isolation vs. connection: He kept his head down for years, alienated friends, ate alone. Javon forces him to connect.
- Naivety vs. awareness: He didn't understand racism, politics, or history. The war educates him brutally.
- His dad: Resentment toward Charles for being absent and arresting people who looked like them, mixed with fear for his safety.

## Arc Summary
- **Start:** A shy, sheltered genius whose entire world is academic achievement. Apolitical. Reserved. Trusts the system because the system rewarded him.
- **Turning Point:** The draft + discovering he's been surveilled by the CIA. The system he trusted is designed to destroy him.
- **Evolution:** Gains physical strength (20 lbs), emotional resilience, and political consciousness through Javon's influence. Falls in love with Avigail. Survives war, cannibalism, near-death.
- **End:** A fundamentally different person. Bearded, hardened, ready to kill. "Let's kill this motherfucker first, and that motherfucker second." The kid who only cared about grades now cares about dismantling the system.

## Key Relationships
- **Javon:** Best friend. Polar opposite. Javon is the catalyst for Darnell's entire transformation - from political awareness to emotional toughness. "We're Harvard brothers."
- **Avigail:** First real romantic connection. Met at the Mamilla Hotel in Jerusalem. Both Harvard-bound for law. She represents hope and normalcy in the chaos.
- **Alec Daheim:** The man who pistol-whipped him. Deep distrust that slowly evolves into reluctant acceptance. Never fully trusts him.
- **Derek:** Childhood friend. Introduced Darnell to weed. Represents the life Darnell left behind.
- **Roxanne:** His closest relationship before Javon. Her death is the first crack in his controlled world.

## Defining Moments
1. Scoring a perfect 1600 on the SAT while grieving his grandmother's death
2. Being cold-shouldered by white classmates after his score was announced - his first real experience of racism
3. Being pistol-whipped by Alec in Philadelphia
4. Running away from his brigade during the sniper attack near Jerusalem
5. Surviving the Jerusalem Forest (bear mauling, trap injuries, cannibalism)
6. Meeting Avigail at the Mamilla Hotel
7. Telling Javon "Let's kill this motherfucker" - the completion of his transformation

## Approved Public Description
> A math prodigy from East Atlanta who scored a perfect 1600 on the SAT and became the first Black student from his school to be accepted to Harvard. Darnell Covington had one goal: become a lawyer. He never cared about politics, history, or the system. He didn't have to. Then the system came for him. Drafted into a war he didn't understand, stripped from the future he built, Darnell discovers that the institution he trusted was designed to destroy people exactly like him. What he does next changes everything.
