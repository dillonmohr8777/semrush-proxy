# Mark ("Longwood")

> Canonical character profile. All public-facing content must align with this.

## Role
- Supporting ally. The rogue weapons engineer. Provides the tactical edge for the final mission.

## Background
- Former CIA agent, code name "Longwood"
- Physics degree (college unknown)
- Worked 2 missions with Alec (relatively insignificant ones)
- Was approached by Garnier anonymously at a gas station in Waterville, CA to join the chip program. Refused.
- Left the CIA voluntarily after ethical concerns about the chip program
- Now lives completely off-grid in a secluded cabin in California
- Built a secret underground weapons facility beneath his cabin
- One missing eye (socket visible), prosthetic arm
- Has a Rottweiler for protection

## The Underground Facility
- Hidden beneath a potbelly stove via hidden hatch
- Descends into a massive compound carved into bedrock
- Contains: phase rifles, blackout units, shielding systems, tactical readouts, encrypted sat maps
- Centerpiece: invisible exoskeleton prototype

## The Exoskeleton
- Self-invented after the CIA's prototype failed
- Renders the wearer completely invisible
- Absorbs anything touching the skin (weapons become invisible too)
- Also absorbs into vehicles - makes entire cars invisible
- Cannot be detected by any known technology
- "Nothing can detect it. No money in the world can detect it."

## Personality
- Unkempt, bearded, rough appearance
- Described as "a freak" by Sabrina
- Enthusiastic about his technology
- Eager to fight ("Fuck that, I want in!")
- ECU-tuned his Jeep Rubicon for an extra 275 HP
- Self-deprecating about his appearance: "look at me"

## Significance
Mark is the proof that the system creates its own enemies. The CIA trained him, used him, then tried to recruit him for evil. He said no, went underground, and built the very weapon that could destroy them. His exoskeleton is the group's only real tactical advantage against Garnier's security.

## Approved Public Description
> They trained him to kill. They asked him to build weapons of control. He said no, disappeared into the California hills, and built something they never imagined: a weapon that makes you invisible. Mark is the ghost the CIA created and couldn't contain.
