# Sabrina ("Echo")

> Canonical character profile. All public-facing content must align with this.

## Role
- Supporting character. Alec's former CIA partner and love interest. A mirror of his journey.

## Background
- From the Midwest (Heartland accent)
- Stanford University - Nuclear Engineering degree
- Went directly into the Navy after college, excelled in combat and weapons training
- Recommended for CIA training at "The Farm" (Camp Peary) - completed 21 weeks
- CIA code name: Echo (later changed to Valkyrie for safety, on Alec's orders)
- Chipped by the government just like Alec - memory erased, turned into an operative

## Physical Description
- Face marked with scars and marks across jaw and forehead "like an old tattoo fading over time"
- Applies makeup to cover rough features
- When chipped: gray/green eyes, pale skin. After chip broken: blue eyes, color returns to face.

## Personality
- Competent and deadly - can match Alec in field work
- Long, drawn-out midwestern accent that she leans into
- Flirtatious and warm with Alec, professional with everyone else
- Uses humor to manage tension
- "Aaron Rodgers R-E-L-A-X" reference - knows exactly how to calm Alec down
- Deeply affected by not remembering her chipping - the lost time haunts her

## The Chip / Memory Loss
- Was chipped at some point after a routine drive with her junior agent Rayne
- Remembers leaving her apartment at 7:44, picking up Rayne, a mysterious handoff in a corridor
- After that: nothing. "She did not remember arriving anywhere after that."
- Experiences headaches and skin sensitivity to sunlight as chip side effects
- Alec broke her chip by headbutting her in his Langley apartment

## Belarus Mission (Backstory)
- Alec and Sabrina's defining shared experience
- Mission to overthrow Belarusian dictator Konstantin Morov
- Sabrina was shot in the back during a firefight in snow-covered forests outside Minsk
- Alec carried her through a blizzard on his back, applied field medicine, saved her life
- The mission failed. Multiple agents killed.
- They had sex for the first time in a slain agent's apartment during recovery
- This cemented their bond beyond professional partnership

## Arc Summary
- **Start:** A chipped CIA operative sent to hunt Javon, with no memory of who she really is
- **Turning Point:** Alec headbutts her, breaking the chip. Her eyes change from gray to blue. She remembers who she is.
- **Evolution:** Becomes the third tactical member alongside Alec and Mark. Struggles with lost memories and sunlight sensitivity.
- **End:** Fully committed to the mission. Ready to kill Garnier alongside Alec.

## Key Relationships
- **Alec:** Deep, complicated love forged through combat. He's the only person who calls her Echo. She's the only person who can humanize him.
- **Avigail:** Initially tense, then protective. Hands her Tylenol, offers comfort.

## Approved Public Description
> Sabrina went from Stanford's nuclear engineering program to the CIA's most dangerous field operations. Code name: Echo. She survived a bullet in Belarus, a failed coup, and years of classified missions. Then someone erased who she was. When she wakes up in a stranger's apartment with a gun in her hand and no memory of how she got there, the only person who can bring her back is the man she thought she'd lost. Now she's fighting a war she doesn't remember starting, alongside people she's only beginning to trust.
