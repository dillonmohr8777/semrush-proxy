# President Ronald McNulty

> Canonical character profile. All public-facing content must align with this.

## Role
- Secondary antagonist. Garnier's puppet. The face of American authoritarianism.

## Background
- Former reality TV star and faux businessman with six bankruptcies
- Father: Greg McNulty, New York City real estate tycoon who ignored him throughout childhood
- Won first term on wit, humor, and public persona with no mandate or political experience
- First term: increasingly cynical, lost reelection to Joseph V. Debs
- Won non-consecutive second term (first since Grover Cleveland in 1892) against even older Debs
- Now running for an illegal third term disguised as his second (via NeuroClick memory manipulation)

## Personality
- Desperate for attention and validation (daddy issues from Greg McNulty ignoring him)
- Speaks in hyperbole and blatant lies as a trademark
- Charismatic in public, insecure in private
- Not as smart as Garnier - doesn't see how he's being used
- "Maybe I should put them all in internment camps like FDR did"
- Uses executive orders to bypass Congress
- Secretly afraid of the violence but hides behind bravado

## Political Actions
- Pulled out of Paris Accord (accelerated climate change)
- Brought back 1930s-style tariffs
- Stacked Supreme Court, secured presidential immunity
- 28th Amendment: gave President power to declare martial law unilaterally
- Used ConnectIt (Garnier's tech) to rig elections via satellite ballot manipulation
- Declared martial law to clear protesters for a church photo-op (held Bible upside down)
- Reinstated the military draft
- Campaigned for war support with 12-day stump speech tour
- Running for illegal third term with public memory erased

## Key Actions in the Plot
- Agreed to Garnier's race-class war plan
- Coordinated with Nelly Ann Longwood on pro-war PR campaign
- Gave speech at Fenway Park announcing draft
- Infected with NeuroClick chip by Garnier via handshake (doesn't know)
- Present at the final confrontation at Garnier's mansion

## The Irony
McNulty is the titular ineptocracy personified. He's incompetent by design - not his own design, but Garnier's. He thinks he's in charge. He's a marionette. "I am the best President of all time, frankly."
