# Podcast Targets

> Researched April 2026. Real, active podcasts organized by priority.

## Tier 1: Best Fit for This Book (Pitch First)

### Fiction/Non/Fiction (Literary Hub)
- **Hosts:** V.V. Ganeshananthan & Whitney Terrell (both novelists)
- **What they cover:** Interpreting current events through literature. Literary Hub podcast.
- **Audience:** Medium-large. Lit Hub platform reach.
- **Angle:** The show's entire premise is examining current events through fiction. This book was made for this show.
- **Where to find:** lithub.com/category/lithubradio/fiction-non-fiction-lithubradio/
- **Status:** [ ] Pitched

### Dystopian Fiction Has Been Moved to Current Affairs
- **Hosts:** Clare Coombe & Masha Yakovleva
- **What they cover:** Politics, ethics, and societal visions of dystopian TV, film, and literature. Biweekly. In association with BeingSociety.com.
- **Audience:** Small-medium, highly engaged.
- **Angle:** Perfect thematic alignment - surveillance, institutional corruption, systemic failure are exactly what this show dissects.
- **Where to find:** dystopianfictioncurrentaffairs.podbean.com
- **Status:** [ ] Pitched

### The Thriller Zone
- **Host:** David Temple (former #1 radio host)
- **What they cover:** Thriller author interviews exclusively. Season 8, 260+ episodes. Self-described "#1 thriller fiction podcast."
- **Audience:** Large. YouTube, iHeart, all major platforms.
- **Angle:** Pitch as a thriller where the antagonist is a system, not a person - a structural twist on the genre.
- **Where to find:** thethrillerzone.com
- **Status:** [ ] Pitched

### Otherppl with Brad Listi
- **Host:** Brad Listi (novelist)
- **What they cover:** Weekly literary podcast. 1,191 episodes since 2011. Guests include George Saunders, Louise Erdrich, Dave Eggers.
- **Audience:** Large. One of the longest-running literary podcasts.
- **Angle:** Literary ambitions within the thriller framework. Race, meritocracy, surveillance through narrative fiction. System-as-villain as structural innovation.
- **Where to find:** otherppl.com
- **Status:** [ ] Pitched

### The Cameron Journal Podcast
- **Host:** Cameron Lee Cowan (author, MFA, political commentator)
- **What they cover:** Political commentary, culture, author interviews. Weekly.
- **Audience:** Small-medium, highly engaged.
- **Angle:** The "ineptocracy" concept will drive an entire conversation. Easiest booking of top targets.
- **Where to find:** cameronjournal.com/podcast
- **Status:** [ ] Pitched

## Outreach Strategy

### Phase 1 (Month 1-2): Build interview reps
- Start with: Literary Lines, The Cameron Journal, In Suspense, Indie Author Addict
- Get comfortable with the format. Record clips to reference in bigger pitches.

### Phase 2 (Month 3-4): Mid-tier pitches
- Pitch: Meet the Thriller Author, No Limits, The Crime Cafe, Writers Ink, Read or Dead
- Reference early interview clips and growing platform.

### Phase 3 (Month 4-6): Top-tier pitches
- Pitch: Fiction/Non/Fiction, The Thriller Zone, Book Riot, Otherppl, Between the Covers
- Send ARC to What Should I Read Next
- Time appearances around launch month.

### Universal Hook
"A political thriller where the system itself is the villain" - works for every show.
