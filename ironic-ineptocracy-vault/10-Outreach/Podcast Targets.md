## Pitch Approach
1. Listen to at least 2 episodes before pitching
2. Reference specific episode or topic in the pitch
3. Frame around what their audience gets, not what you get
4. Lead with the book's themes and relevance, not "I wrote a book"
5. Offer talking points that match their style
6. See `Outreach Templates.md` for message templates

## Talking Points (Universal)
- What "ineptocracy" means and why it's ironic
- How the system (not a person) can be the villain
- Writing about race and power without being preachy
- Why political fiction matters more than political commentary
- The process of building a world that feels uncomfortably real
