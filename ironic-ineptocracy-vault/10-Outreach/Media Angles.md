# Media Angles

> Newsworthy angles for press coverage.

## Primary Angles

### 1. "The Novel That Names What Everyone's Thinking About Institutions"
- Timeliness: institutional trust at historic lows
- Hook: Fiction as a lens for systemic critique
- Best for: Cultural commentary outlets, opinion sections

### 2. "Why a Marketing Operator Wrote the Most Politically Honest Thriller of the Year"
- Timeliness: Author background + unique perspective
- Hook: Not a career novelist - someone who sees systems from inside
- Best for: Business/marketing publications, author profiles

### 3. "Debut Author Builds AI-Powered Marketing Machine to Launch Novel"
- Timeliness: AI + creative industries
- Hook: The meta-story of how the book is being marketed
- Best for: Tech/marketing publications, writing industry outlets

### 4. "Political Fiction's New Wave: Thrillers That Go Beyond Conspiracies"
- Timeliness: Genre evolution
- Hook: Trend piece featuring the book alongside others
- Best for: Literary publications, book review sections

## Press Kit Contents
- [ ] Author bio (short + long)
