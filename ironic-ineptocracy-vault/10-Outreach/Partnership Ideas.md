# Partnership Ideas

> Strategic partnerships beyond standard reviewer outreach.

## Book Club Partnerships
- Offer discussion guide + author Q&A for clubs that choose the book
- Target: online book clubs focused on political fiction or social commentary

## Independent Bookstore Partnerships
- Signed copies, author events, shelf cards
- Focus on bookstores that champion debut authors or political fiction

## Academic / Course Partnerships
- Political science courses exploring institutional critique
- Creative writing programs studying genre-blending
- Media literacy classes

## Cross-Author Partnerships
- Joint newsletter swaps with authors in similar lanes
- Co-hosted Instagram Lives or Twitter Spaces
- Shared "if you liked X, try Y" content

## Organization Partnerships
- Civil liberties organizations (thematic alignment)
- Media literacy groups
- Anti-corruption nonprofits
- Writing communities

## Rules
- Every partnership must be genuine alignment, not just reach
- The book's themes should resonate with the partner's audience
- Never compromise the book's positioning for a partnership
