# Substack Targets

> Researched April 2026. Real Substack writers organized by priority.

## Tier 1: Highest Alignment (Pitch First)

### Dystopian Fiction Has Been Moved to Current Affairs
- **Writers:** Clare Coombe & Masha Yakovleva
- **URL:** dystopianfictioncurrentaffairs.substack.com
- **What they cover:** Politics, ethics, and societal visions of dystopian fiction. Fortnightly newsletter + podcast.
- **Subscribers:** Niche but highly targeted
- **Collaboration:** Podcast appearance or newsletter feature. Their entire premise is that dystopian fiction IS current affairs.
- **Pitch:** "A thriller about designed incompetence: when the dystopia is the bureaucracy itself."
- **Status:** [ ] Pitched

### The.Ink - Anand Giridharadas
- **URL:** the.ink
- **What they cover:** How power works, institutional corruption, inequality. Author of "Winners Take All." Runs book club featuring underrepresented voices.
- **Subscribers:** Hundreds of thousands
- **Collaboration:** The Ink Book Club selection or author interview. His format of "the hardest questions about money and power" aligns perfectly.
- **Pitch:** This novel is a fictional companion to his nonfiction work on how elites maintain corrupt systems.
- **Status:** [ ] Pitched

### Counter Craft - Lincoln Michel
- **URL:** countercraft.substack.com
- **What they cover:** Fiction craft, publishing, weird books, literary analysis. Former editor-in-chief of Electric Literature.
- **Subscribers:** Thousands (top-ranked fiction newsletter)
- **Collaboration:** Guest essay on craft - "Writing a political thriller that takes institutions seriously" or where genre and literary fiction intersect.
- **Pitch:** Genre-bending fiction that's both entertaining and intellectually rigorous.
- **Status:** [ ] Pitched

### Get It Write - Andrea Bartz
- **URL:** andibartz.substack.com
- **What they cover:** Author interviews, writing craft, publishing intel. Bartz is NYT bestselling thriller author.
- **Subscribers:** Thousands, growing
- **Collaboration:** Author interview about craft and publishing journey. Her audience is thriller readers AND writers.
- **Status:** [ ] Pitched

### The Real Book Spy - Ryan Steck
- **URL:** therealbookspy.substack.com
- **What they cover:** THE authority on thriller fiction. 1,000+ author interviews. Publishes annual "100 Best Mysteries & Thrillers."
- **Subscribers:** Thousands (millions across platforms)
- **Collaboration:** Review or author interview. A recommendation from Steck carries major weight.
- **Status:** [ ] Pitched

## Tier 2: Strong Fit (Pitch Next)

### Thinking about... - Timothy Snyder
- **URL:** snyder.substack.com
- **What they cover:** Democracy, authoritarianism, fascism, institutional corruption. Yale historian. Author of "On Tyranny."
- **Subscribers:** 446,000+
- **Collaboration:** Guest essay: "A novelist's perspective on how designed incompetence enables authoritarianism."
- **Why this fits:** Snyder's core thesis - corruption destroys democratic institutions from within - is literally the premise of this novel.
- **Status:** [ ] Pitched

### Persuasion - Yascha Mounk
- **URL:** persuasion.community
- **What they cover:** Liberal democracy, meritocracy debate, institutional reform. Atlantic contributing writer.
- **Subscribers:** 239,000+
- **Collaboration:** Guest essay or podcast. "What fiction reveals about the failure of meritocracy."
- **Why this fits:** Mounk actively debates meritocracy - a central theme of the book.
- **Status:** [ ] Pitched

### The Audacity - Roxane Gay
- **URL:** audacity.substack.com
- **What they cover:** Cultural criticism, the world we live in. Runs The Audacious Book Club for underrepresented writers. Publishes emerging writers biweekly.
- **Subscribers:** Hundreds of thousands
- **Collaboration:** Audacious Book Club selection or guest essay slot.
- **Why this fits:** Gay engages directly with race, power, cultural systems.
- **Status:** [ ] Pitched

### Thriller Thursday - Multi-author collective
- **URL:** thrillerthursday.substack.com
- **What they cover:** Writing processes, reading habits, recommendations from established thriller authors (USA Today bestsellers, Edgar winners).
- **Collaboration:** Guest recommendation slot or author spotlight.
- **Why this fits:** Direct-to-thriller-reader pipeline.
- **Status:** [ ] Pitched

### FictionMatters - Sara Hildreth
- **URL:** fictionmatters.substack.com
- **What they cover:** Reflective book recommendations. 100K+ Instagram. MA in English from Georgetown.
- **Collaboration:** Weekly recommendations or "This Month in Books" podcast.
- **Why this fits:** Audience reads fiction thoughtfully, wants books that reward analysis.
- **Status:** [ ] Pitched
