# Outreach Templates

> Pre-written templates for different outreach contexts. Personalize every time.

## Podcast Pitch
```
Subject: Guest pitch - political fiction, systems, and why incompetence is designed

Hi [Name],

I'm a fan of [specific episode reference] - especially the part about [specific detail].

I'm the author of The Ironic Ineptocracy, a political thriller that explores what happens when institutional failure isn't an accident but a feature of the system. The novel examines meritocracy, surveillance, and race through characters who discover the machinery operating beneath their world.

I think your audience would connect with a conversation about [specific angle relevant to their show]:
- Why the "system as villain" is the next evolution of political fiction
- Writing about race and power without being preachy
- How fiction can name things journalism can't

Happy to share an ARC if you're interested.

Best,
Dillon Mohr
[website] | [social]
```

## Rules
- ALWAYS personalize the bracketed sections
- NEVER send a template without customizing for the recipient
- Keep it under 150 words
- Lead with their work, not yours
- One clear ask per message
