# Guest Post & Author Essay Targets

> Real sites researched April 2026. Each accepts guest contributions from authors.

## Tier 1: Highest Impact (Pitch First)

### CrimeReads
- **URL:** https://crimereads.com
- **What they publish:** Essays, features, lists, cultural criticism about crime/mystery/thriller literature. Regularly publishes author essays tied to new books.
- **Guidelines:** Pitch via email. Full essays welcome. Sister site to Lit Hub.
- **Authority:** High DA, high traffic. Under the Lit Hub umbrella.
- **Contact:** info@lithub.com
- **Best pitch:** "The real-world surveillance state as thriller fuel" or "Writing corruption you can't exaggerate: how institutional dysfunction outpaces fiction"
- **Status:** [ ] Pitched

### Literary Hub (Lit Hub)
- **URL:** https://lithub.com
- **What they publish:** Essays connecting literature to wider culture, critical essays, personal essays on writing life
- **Guidelines:** Send 2-3 paragraph pitch explaining what makes the story interesting, why it matters now, what perspective you bring. Full finished essays also accepted. No fiction/poetry - essays and nonfiction only.
- **Authority:** DA 80+. Ranked #51 globally in Books & Literature. Millions of monthly visits.
- **Contact:** info@lithub.com
- **Best pitch:** "Why political fiction matters more than ever in the age of institutional distrust" - connecting the novel's themes to the current cultural moment
- **Status:** [ ] Pitched

### Chicago Review of Books
- **URL:** https://chireviewofbooks.com
- **What they publish:** Reviews (600-800 words), interviews (1,200-1,500 words), author features, personal/craft essays, book lists. Specifically invites authors with upcoming releases to write directly to readers.
- **Guidelines:** Pitch via email. Include short bio and clips. Interested in small press/under-the-radar titles, BIPOC and LGBTQ+ authors, works that put books in conversation with culture.
- **Authority:** DA 55-65.
- **Contact:** chireviewofbooks@gmail.com
- **Best pitch:** Author feature essay - "Why I wrote a political thriller about the systems we pretend are working"
- **Status:** [ ] Pitched

### Electric Literature
- **URL:** https://electricliterature.com
- **What they publish:** Cultural criticism, personal narratives, craft essays, book-related cultural commentary
- **Guidelines:** Cultural criticism pitches accepted year-round via email. Essays 3,500 words or less. Personal narrative essays (2,000-6,500 words) via Submittable during open periods. No AI content.
- **Authority:** DA 70+.
- **Contact:** editors@electricliterature.com (subject line "PITCH:" followed by title)
- **Best pitch:** Cultural criticism essay on "The myth of meritocracy in American political fiction"
- **Status:** [ ] Pitched

## Tier 2: Strong Fit (Pitch Next)

### Guernica
- **URL:** https://www.guernicamag.com
- **What they publish:** Essays, memoir, criticism, journalism at the intersection of arts and politics. Award-winning.
- **Guidelines:** No pitches - only completed manuscripts via Submittable. 2,500+ words (most published 4,000-5,000). Previously unpublished only. Response ~6 months. Pays contributors.
- **Authority:** DA 65+. Prestigious literary magazine.
- **Contact:** Submittable portal at guernicamagazine.submittable.com/submit
- **Best pitch:** Completed essay: "Surveillance, race, and the architecture of institutional failure" - arts-meets-politics mission is perfect alignment
- **Status:** [ ] Submitted

### Longreads
- **URL:** https://longreads.com
- **What they publish:** Original nonfiction - personal essays, reported features, reading lists, critical essays. 2,000-6,000 words.
- **Guidelines:** Personal essays: submit full drafts. Reported features: 2-3 paragraph pitch with writing samples. No AI content. Pays contributors (Net-60).
- **Authority:** Very high DA. Part of WordPress/Automattic family.
- **Contact:** hello@longreads.com
- **Best pitch:** "How political thrillers predict institutional collapse before the headlines do"
- **Status:** [ ] Pitched

### The Rumpus
- **URL:** https://therumpus.net
- **What they publish:** Voice-driven personal essays and non-traditional nonfiction. Up to 5,000 words.
- **Guidelines:** Submit via Submittable. Previously unpublished. Response ~3 months. Pays $500 for prose.
- **Authority:** DA 70+.
- **Contact:** therumpus.submittable.com/submit
- **Best pitch:** Personal essay on the experience of writing about race and institutional power as a debut novelist
- **Status:** [ ] Submitted

### Jane Friedman
- **URL:** https://janefriedman.com

- **Guidelines:** Minimum 800 words, average 1,000-2,000. Informational content about writing/publishing. Must offer fresh perspective. Year-round submissions.
- **Authority:** DA 70+.
- **Contact:** janefriedman.com/guest-posts/ (submission form)
- **Best pitch:** "What I learned about the publishing landscape as a debut author tackling politically charged themes"

- **Authority:** DA 55-65.
- **Contact:** writerunboxed@writerunboxed.com

- **URL:** https://www.writersdigest.com

- **Guidelines:** 1,200-word guest columns. Focus on instructional/how-to. Pays $0.50/word for print, $50-100 for online. Response 2-4 months.
- **Authority:** DA 80+.
- **Contact:** writers.digest@aimmedia.com
- **Best pitch:** "How to research and write authentic institutional corruption for fiction" - practical craft article

- **Guidelines:** Guest posts accepted. No payment but bio with up to two promotional links.
- **Authority:** DA 55-65.
- **Contact:** writershelpingwriters.net/guest-post-policy/

### The Big Thrill (International Thriller Writers)
