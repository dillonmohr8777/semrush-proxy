# Book Reviewers

> Researched April 2026. Real reviewers and outlets organized by priority and timeline.

## Tier 1: Professional Review Outlets (Submit 4+ Months Before Pub)

### Shelf Awareness
- **Platform:** Daily trade newsletter to booksellers and librarians
- **Audience:** 750,000+ readers
- **How to submit:** Email galleys to galleys@shelf-awareness.com. Title / Author / ISBN / Pub Date. 4 months before pub. Must be available through Ingram.
- **Cost:** Free
- **Requirement:** Ingram distribution required. No ebook-only or POD.
- **Status:** [ ] Submitted

### Kirkus Indie Reviews
- **Platform:** Trade review (print + online)
- **Audience:** 50,000+ industry influencers. Massive librarian/bookseller reach.
- **How to submit:** kirkusreviews.com/indie-reviews/. Upload final PDF/Word.
- **Cost:** Standard $425 (7-9 weeks). Express $575 (4-6 weeks).
- **Note:** Can choose not to publish negative reviews. Strong reviews featured in print/newsletter.
- **Status:** [ ] Submitted

### Publishers Weekly / BookLife
- **Platform:** Largest trade publication in publishing
- **How to submit:** Two options: PW Review Consideration via BookLife ($25, not guaranteed) or BookLife Reviews ($399 guaranteed, 6 weeks).
- **Status:** [ ] Submitted

### Foreword Reviews / Clarion Reviews
- **Platform:** Trade review magazine, indie/small press focus
- **Audience:** 20,000+ industry readers
- **How to submit:** Clarion (paid, guaranteed): $499, 4-6 weeks. Free editorial review: send copy 2+ months before pub date (not guaranteed).
- **Contact:** publishers.forewordreviews.com/reviews/clarion/
- **Status:** [ ] Submitted

### BookPage
- **Platform:** Book reviews distributed to libraries and bookstores nationwide
- **Authority:** DA 70+
- **How to submit:** E-galleys to relevant editor. Savanna Walker (genre/thriller) or Phoebe Farrell-Sherman (literary fiction).
- **Contact:** savanna@bookpage.com or phoebe@bookpage.com
- **Status:** [ ] Submitted

## Review Platforms / Services

### NetGalley
- **What:** Digital ARC distribution to reviewers (librarians, bloggers, media, Goodreads reviewers)
- **Cost:** ~$499 for 6-month listing (IBPA discount: $349). Co-op as low as $45-79/month.
- **Goal:** Seed 20-50 early reviews that cross-post to Goodreads and Amazon.
- **Status:** [ ] Listed

### Reedsy Discovery
- **What:** Review platform + weekly newsletter (270,000+ subscribers, 2,000+ reviewers)
- **Cost:** $50
- **Status:** [ ] Listed

### BookSirens
- **What:** Reviewer matching service. ~1,000 vetted blogs, cumulative 1M+ readers.
- **How:** Filter by genre (political thriller), platform, indie acceptance.
- **Status:** [ ] Listed
