# Ad Creative Angles

> For when paid promotion becomes part of the strategy (Tier 3 - requires direct approval).

## Creative Angles

### Angle 1: The Question
- "What if the system isn't broken?"
- Visual: Institutional building, slightly distorted
- CTA: Learn more / Read the first chapter

### Angle 2: The Comp
- "For fans of [COMP] who want something deeper"
- Visual: Book cover + atmospheric background
- CTA: Get your copy

### Angle 3: The Quote
- [Powerful line from the book]
- Visual: Text-forward design, brand colors
- CTA: Discover the novel

### Angle 4: The Relevance
- "The most relevant political thriller of the year"
- Visual: Headline-style treatment
- CTA: Read the first chapter free

## Platform-Specific Notes
| Platform | Best Format | Audience Targeting |
|----------|-----------|-------------------|
| Meta (Facebook/Instagram) | Image + copy, video | Interest: political fiction, thrillers, similar authors |
| Amazon Ads | Sponsored product, lockscreen | Genre keywords, comp authors |
| BookBub | Featured deal, CPC ads | Genre targeting |
| Reddit | Promoted post | Relevant subreddits |

## Budget Notes
- NO ad spend without explicit approval
- Start small ($5-10/day) and test angles
- Measure: CPC, CTR, conversion to purchase/signup
- Kill underperforming creative after 3 days
