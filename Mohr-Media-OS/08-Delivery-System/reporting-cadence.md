# Reporting Cadence

## Daily

- Internal standup note per client in the client channel
- 10 minutes or less
- One line per role on what moved yesterday, what moves today, blockers

## Weekly

- Client weekly report delivered every Friday afternoon
- Format: template in `05-Client-Operating-System/weekly-report-template.md`
- Includes pipeline impact, shipped items, experiments, blockers, next week focus

## Monthly

- Monthly review with economic buyer
- Format: template in `05-Client-Operating-System/monthly-review-template.md`
- First business day of the new month

## Quarterly

- Quarterly business review
- Agenda:
  1. Scorecard vs targets
  2. Offer review with offer-architect
  3. Channel review
  4. Renewal and expansion conversation
- Delivered in person or on live video when possible

## Internal Mohr OS reporting

- Weekly operator review every Monday (see `03-SOP-Library/weekly-review.md`)
- Monthly system review first business day of month
- Quarterly vault synthesis from memory-engine

## Rules

- No client report skipped for any reason
- Bad news never waits for the next scheduled report
- Every report ends with a named next action and owner
