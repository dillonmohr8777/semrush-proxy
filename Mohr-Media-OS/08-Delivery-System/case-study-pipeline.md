# Case Study Pipeline

Case studies are not marketing assets. They are sales closers, retention anchors, and offer proof. They are produced on a schedule, not as an afterthought.

## When to start a case study

- Day 60 of any engagement where metrics are on target
- Immediately after a sprint that produced an outsized win
- At renewal for any client on target for 6 months or more

## Production pipeline

1. **Day 0**: execution-engine flags client as case study eligible
2. **Day 3**: outline drafted by content-machine using the case study template
3. **Day 7**: numbers verified with client in writing
4. **Day 10**: draft written and humanized
5. **Day 14**: client review and approval
6. **Day 17**: visuals produced
7. **Day 21**: publish on owned surface
8. **Day 22**: distribute across channels
9. **Day 30**: measure pipeline attribution and ingest to vault

```
- Numbers at start:

- System 2
- System 3

## What changed
| Metric | Before | After | Delta |
|---|---|---|---|
| ... | ... | ... | ... |

## The unlock
One paragraph on the single biggest lever.

## What the client says
Direct quote.

## What the client now owns
Runbooks, systems, dashboards, assets.
```

## Rules

- Every number in a case study is sourced and client approved
- No case study ships without the client's written signoff
- Every case study is versioned when numbers update
- Losing case studies get written too and stored privately as internal proof
