# Retention Playbook

Retention is the most profitable revenue in Mohr Media. This playbook extends client life.

## Retention triggers to watch

| Signal | Severity | Response |
|---|---|---|
| Missed demo without reschedule | Medium | Direct check in within 24 hours |
| Two weeks without client reply | Medium | Economic buyer call |
| Metric below target for 2 sprints | High | Root cause diagnostic and reset |
| Champion leaves the company | High | Immediate re map of stakeholders |
| New competitor agency pitching | High | Offer review and proof refresh |
| Price renegotiation request | High | Offer-architect review |

## The 90 day retention arc

- Day 30: Month 1 review (see template), set clear month 2 target
- Day 60: Month 2 review, start case study draft if metrics support it
- Day 75: Renewal conversation prep
- Day 85: Renewal conversation
- Day 90: Renewal or clean offboarding

## Expansion ladder

1. Add a second offer tier
2. Add an adjacent channel (for example add paid to a pipeline only engagement)
3. Add a second brand or business unit
4. Add an advisory retainer on top of the install

## Rules

- Retention conversations are scheduled, not reactive
- Expansion is only proposed after a clear month of on target metrics
- No silent discounts. Every concession maps to a scope change.
