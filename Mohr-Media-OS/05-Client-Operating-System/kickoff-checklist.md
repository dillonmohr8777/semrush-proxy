# Kickoff Checklist

## Before kickoff

- [ ] Welcome email sent within 24 hours of signature
- [ ] Client profile created in `05-Client-Operating-System/clients/`
- [ ] Client entity created in Obsidian vault
- [ ] Contract stored in `contract.md`
- [ ] Kickoff prep packet delivered 48 hours before call
- [ ] Access list assembled (ad accounts, CRM, email, analytics, CMS)
- [ ] Shared channel opened

## Kickoff call agenda 75 minutes

1. Outcomes and success metrics confirmed (10 min)
2. Stakeholders and decision process (10 min)
3. Offer, ICP, and current motion walkthrough (15 min)
4. Delivery spec draft review (15 min)
5. Sprint 1 preview (10 min)
6. Access and tool handoff (10 min)
7. Communication cadence and working agreement (5 min)

## Deliverables by end of kickoff

- Signed delivery spec
- Sprint 1 plan posted
- Access list complete or scheduled within 5 business days
- Working agreement signed
- Weekly report cadence confirmed

## Failure signals

- Economic buyer does not attend
- Success metric cannot be put in numbers
- Access list missing critical tools and no plan to get them
- Client already uses three competing agencies

Any failure signal escalates to orchestrator before sprint 1 launches.
