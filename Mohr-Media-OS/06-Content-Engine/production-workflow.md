# Production Workflow

## Stages

1. Topic selection from calendar
2. Outline with prompt CP-02 or CP-03
3. Draft with prompt CP-03
4. Humanize with prompt CP-04
5. Repurpose with prompt CP-05
6. Distribute with prompt CP-06
7. Measure at day 14 and day 30
8. Ingest into memory-engine

## Weekly batch cadence

- Monday: topic selection and outlines for the week
- Tuesday: long form drafts
- Wednesday: humanize and review
- Thursday: short form derivatives
- Friday: schedule distribution and brief next week

## Quality gate

Every draft goes through a rubric before shipping.

| Check | Pass criteria |
|---|---|
| Hook | Promises a specific outcome in 15 words or less |
| Proof | Within first scroll |
| Structure | Short paragraphs, no filler, no AI tells |
| Voice | Matches brand voice reference |
| CTA | Exactly one, tracked, tied to offer |
| Claims | Every claim has a source or proof |
| Style | No em dashes, no corporate hedges, no emojis unless client brand demands |

Any fail sends the draft back to CP-03 or CP-04.

## Production capacity

A single operator using Mohr OS should produce:

- 2 long form pieces per week
- 10 short form posts per week
- 1 thread per week
- 2 video scripts per week
- All with distribution plans shipped

If output is below that line, the workflow is broken. Do not blame the operator.
