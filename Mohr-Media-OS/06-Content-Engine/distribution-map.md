# Distribution Map

The channels, the formats, the cadence, and the handoffs.

## Channels

| Channel | Format | Cadence | Goal | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Owned blog | Long form | 2 per week | SEO plus authority | content-machine |
| Newsletter | Long form | 1 per week | Nurture and conversion | content-machine |
| LinkedIn | Short form, document, carousel | 5 per week | Pipeline plus inbound | content-machine |
| X | Thread, short post | 1 thread per week plus daily short posts | Audience and proof | content-machine |
| YouTube | Long form video, shorts | 1 long per week, 3 shorts per week | Mid funnel nurture | content-machine |
| Podcast guesting | Interview | 2 per month | Authority and backlinks | orchestrator |
| Cold email drops | Value drop sequence | Per campaign | Reply lift | pipeline-builder |
| Sales enablement | Proof packets | Per deal | Close support | closer-engine |
| Paid amplification | Boosted posts and ads | Per winner | Scale winners | ad-strategist |

## Cross channel rules

1. Long form lands first on owned surface and then syndicates
2. Every long form produces at least 5 social derivatives within 48 hours
3. Winning LinkedIn posts over a defined threshold flow to ad-strategist for paid amplification
4. Every piece links back to one named offer
5. No piece ships without a tracking link

## Measurement

- Pipeline dollars attributed within 30 days of publish
- Meetings booked from the piece
- Reply lift when used in outbound
- Ranking if SEO pillar

## Kill rules

- A channel that produces no pipeline in 60 days is paused
- A format that produces no pipeline in 90 days is dropped
- A pillar that produces no pipeline in a quarter is replaced
