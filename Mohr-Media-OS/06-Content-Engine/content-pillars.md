# Content Pillars

Mohr Media runs on five pillars. Every piece maps to one pillar. No pillar runs forever. Pillars are reviewed quarterly.

## Pillar 1: Systems over campaigns

Core belief: operators win by installing machines, not renting tactics.
Pain addressed: burnout on one off campaigns with no compounding.
Offer tie: Tier 3 Full Revenue OS.
Formats: long form essays, screen recordings, case studies.

## Pillar 2: Pipeline math

Core belief: most agencies hide behind vanity metrics because they do not understand pipeline arithmetic.
Pain addressed: operators who cannot forecast revenue from marketing spend.
Offer tie: Tier 1 Offer and Pipeline Install.
Formats: short posts with calculators, thread breakdowns, spreadsheets.

## Pillar 3: Offer engineering

Core belief: the offer is the leverage point. Bad offers cannot be fixed by better copy.
Pain addressed: operators who blame channels when the real issue is the offer.
Offer tie: every tier.
Formats: teardown threads, before and after posts, template drops.

## Pillar 4: Paid media payback

Core belief: ads that do not pay back inside 90 days are a subsidy to a platform.
Pain addressed: operators bleeding money on agencies who chase ROAS as a score.
Offer tie: Tier 2 Paid Media and CRO Install.
Formats: campaign teardowns, payback math, CRO audits in public.

## Pillar 5: The second brain

Core belief: knowledge that does not get captured gets re derived. Re derivation is a tax.
Pain addressed: operators who lose every insight their team generates.
Offer tie: Tier 3 Full Revenue OS.
Formats: workflow walkthroughs, vault screenshots, memory-engine deep dives.

## Review

Every quarter the orchestrator kills at least one pillar and replaces it if evidence demands it. Pillar performance is judged by content to pipeline rate, not by engagement.
