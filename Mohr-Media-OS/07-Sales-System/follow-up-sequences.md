# Follow Up Sequences

## Post discovery before proposal

T + 2 hours: recap email with diagnosis and success picture.
T + 24 hours: proposal sent.

## Post proposal

T + 1 day: confirmation email "Did the proposal land?"
T + 3 days: value add "Thought about your situation overnight, here is a thing I would do this week regardless of whether we work together."
T + 7 days: direct "Are we moving forward or not? Happy to hear either answer."
T + 14 days: breakup "I will stop following up after this. If the answer is not now, tell me when is and I will follow up then."
T + 45 days: reactivation nudge if no response "Circling back. Here is a new data point since we spoke."

## After a lost deal

T + 30 days: insight share "Here is something I learned since we talked that reminded me of your situation."
T + 90 days: new case study drop "A similar company just finished the install. Here is what happened."
T + 180 days: status check "Still thinking about the problem we discussed?"

## After a won deal, from closer-engine back to prospect

T + 30 days: first results share "Here is what the first 30 days looked like, just so you can see what your install looks like at the same stage."
T + 90 days: case study ask "Results are strong enough that I want to tell this story. Can we put your name on it?"

## Rules

- Every touch adds value or removes friction. No empty check ins.
- No guilt. No urgency fakes. No fake scarcity.
- Every sequence is shorter than the deal cycle it supports.
- Every reply from a follow up updates the deal record immediately.
