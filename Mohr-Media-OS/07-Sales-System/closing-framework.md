# Closing Framework

The Mohr OS closing framework is called PROOF.

## P. Point to the diagnosis

Restate the pain in their language. "You said you are spending 12k per month on paid and not recovering it inside 90 days."

## R. Reframe the gap

Show the delta between current state and success picture in numbers. "You want to be at 180k in pipeline per month. Today you are at 60k. That is a 120k gap every month."

## O. Offer the mechanism

Name the mechanism and why it is different. "The way we close that gap is by installing three systems: {{system}}, {{system}}, {{system}}. That is not a campaign. That is a machine you own."

## O. Open the proof

Drop the proof block: case study, named outcome, metric. "Here is a client in a similar spot 90 days ago. They moved from {{before}} to {{after}}. Here is the exact offer we ran for them."

## F. Forward motion

Ask the direct question. "If we install this starting {{date}}, you would hit your number by {{deadline}}. Are you ready to move forward today?"

## The three possible outcomes

1. **Yes**. Send contract inside 24 hours. Handoff to execution-engine.
2. **Not yet**. Diagnose the blocker. Re enter discovery on the specific objection. Rebook within 7 days.
3. **No**. Log the loss reason immediately. Disqualify with grace. Ask for one referral.

## Hard rules

- Never close without restating the diagnosis
- Never send a proposal to someone who cannot describe their own success picture
- Never lower the price without changing the scope
- Never ghost a no. Log it and ingest it.
