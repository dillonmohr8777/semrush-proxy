# Discovery Call Script

45 minute call. Built on the Mohr OS discovery framework.

## Opening 3 minutes

"Thanks for making time. My goal today is to understand where you are, figure out if there is a fit, and if there is, walk you through what an engagement would look like. If there is not, I will tell you. Does that work?"

## Context 5 minutes

- What prompted you to take this call?
- What changed in the last 30 days?
- What were you hoping to get out of this conversation?

Listen for trigger events. Write down the first numeric detail they share.

## Current state 10 minutes

- Walk me through how you currently generate revenue today.
- Where do your best customers come from?
- What does your sales motion look like?
- What is your current close rate and ACV?
- What are you spending on marketing today?

Goal: capture the math in writing during the call.

## Cost of inaction 7 minutes

- If nothing changes for the next 90 days, what does that look like?
- What is the cost of that in dollars?
- What is the cost of that in people or stress?
- What do you lose if this problem is still unsolved in 6 months?

Do not rush this section. Silence is your friend here.

## Success picture 7 minutes

- If we fast forward 90 days and it worked, what is true?
- What number would make you say this was worth it?
- Who else at the company needs to see that number?

## Decision process 5 minutes

- Who signs a decision like this?
- Who else needs to be in the room?
- How have you decided on similar investments before?
- What would disqualify us in your view?

## Close 5 minutes

Three possible closes based on what you heard:

1. **Fit and urgency present**: "Based on what you told me, here is what I would install. Can we book a 30 minute working session to walk you through the proposal on {{day}}?"
2. **Fit present, urgency missing**: "This is a fit, but you are not ready to move yet. Let me send you one resource and follow up in {{n}} weeks."
3. **Not a fit**: "I do not think we are the right fit because {{reason}}. Here is who you should talk to instead."

## Hard rules

- Never pitch during discovery
- Never quote a price live unless asked directly and your hypothesis is firm
- Always end the call with a booked next step or a clear disqualification
