# Objection Handling

Every objection gets a scripted response. Improvising is how deals get lost.

## Price

**"It is too expensive."**

Reframe: "Compared to what? Let us walk the math. Your ACV is {{acv}} and your close rate is {{cr}}. If this install lifts close rate by even 5 points, the install pays for itself inside {{days}} days. Is that math wrong?"

**"Can you do it for less?"**

Reframe: "I can change the scope, but I will not change the price for the same scope. Which part of the install do you want to drop?"

## Timing

**"We are not ready yet."**

Reframe: "Help me understand what has to be true for you to be ready. Is it a number, a person, or a season?"

**"Let us revisit in 90 days."**

Reframe: "What will be different in 90 days that is not true today? If the answer is nothing, the real issue is not timing."

## Internal politics

**"I need to run this by my partner/board."**

Reframe: "Completely fair. Let me send you the one page version they will ask for. What is the number they will care about most?"

**"My team is not convinced."**

Reframe: "Who specifically is not convinced, and what is their specific concern? Let me address it directly."

## Lack of proof

**"Do you have case studies in my industry?"**

Reframe: "The mechanism works across industries because the bottleneck you described is a math problem, not an industry problem. Here is the closest case. Here is the math that proves the mechanism generalizes."

## Fit doubt

**"We tried an agency before and it did not work."**

Reframe: "I believe you. Walk me through what they did and why it failed. I need to make sure we are not walking into the same trap."

## Past agency trauma

**"We got burned last time."**

Reframe: "That is the reason Mohr Media does not operate as a retainer agency. We install systems you own at the end. Your risk goes to zero because the asset stays with you."

## Rules

- Always repeat the objection back in writing before responding
- Never interrupt the objection
- Never pair two objections into one answer
- If the same objection clusters across 3 losses, hand it to offer-architect
