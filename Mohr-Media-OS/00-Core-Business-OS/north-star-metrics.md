# North Star Metrics

Mohr Media operates against a fixed set of numbers. Everything the OS produces is graded against these.

## Primary north star

Qualified pipeline dollars created per week.

Definition: total contract value of opportunities that have passed discovery and entered proposal stage, created in the last 7 days, across all active engagements.

## Secondary metrics

| Metric | Definition | Target direction |
|---|---|---|
| Qualified conversations booked | Calls with ICP match and budget signal | Up |
| Discovery to proposal rate | Percent of discovery calls that advance | Up |
| Proposal to close rate | Percent of proposals that convert | Up |
| Average contract value | Total closed revenue divided by deal count | Up |
| Cycle time | Days from first conversation to closed won | Down |
| Client retention months | Average months a client stays active | Up |
| Content to pipeline rate | Pipeline dollars attributable to content within 30 days | Up |
| Paid media payback | Days until ad spend is recovered by closed revenue | Down |

## Anti metrics

Metrics we explicitly refuse to optimize for:

- Impressions
- Follower count without conversion path
- Email open rate as a standalone goal
- Engagement rate decoupled from pipeline
- Clicks without attribution
- Posting frequency as a goal

## Reporting cadence

- Weekly operator review every Monday
- Monthly system review first business day of month
- Quarterly offer and positioning review
