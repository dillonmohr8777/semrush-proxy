# Operating Principles

These are the non negotiable principles that govern how Mohr OS operates.

## 1. Revenue is the only scoreboard

If a task, asset, or meeting does not move pipeline, conversations, deals, or retention, it does not ship.

## 2. Systems beat campaigns

Anything that runs once is a failure of design. Every asset is engineered to be reused, tuned, and compounded.

## 3. Install, do not rent

Clients must finish each engagement with a working machine they own. Mohr Media is not a dependency, it is a transfer of capability.

## 4. Operator first, audience second

Every decision prioritizes the operator's revenue reality over creative vanity, platform trends, or agency fashion.

## 5. Distribution outranks production

A great asset with weak distribution loses to a good asset with strong distribution every single time. Always plan distribution before production.

## 6. One offer, one ICP, one motion at a time

Complexity kills conversion. Mohr OS forces narrowing before scaling.

## 7. Proof before claims

No positioning claim ships without a named outcome, metric, before, and after.

## 8. Compounding over novelty

The content engine, the pipeline engine, and the second brain all exist to compound. Novelty is a tax.

## 9. Write it down or it did not happen

Every insight, win, loss, and experiment lands inside the memory engine. The Obsidian vault is the source of truth.

## 10. Speed is a feature

Velocity wins deals. Delivery sprints are time boxed. Experiments are time boxed. Decisions are time boxed.
