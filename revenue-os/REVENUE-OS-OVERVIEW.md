# Revenue OS — Your Sales Leadership Operating System

## What Is This?

Revenue OS is a private command center built specifically for you as VP of Sales. It sits on top of HubSpot and gives you something HubSpot alone cannot: **structured thinking, proactive intelligence, and leadership-ready visibility** — without opening 20 deal records or building another spreadsheet.

Think of it as a second brain for revenue leadership.

It has four layers:

| Layer | What It Does | Tool |
|-------|-------------|------|
| **Storage & Memory** | Stores every deal note, pipeline review, coaching session, forecast, and leadership memo in one organized, searchable system | Obsidian |
| **Live Data** | Pulls real-time deal data, activities, stages, close dates, and rep behavior directly from your CRM | HubSpot API |
| **Intelligence** | Six AI agents analyze pipeline risk, forecast confidence, deal health, rep performance, and generate executive summaries | Claude / Agents |
| **Delivery** | Sends you daily briefs, risk alerts, coaching flags, and forecast drift warnings — automatically | Email + HubSpot Tasks |

**You don't have to change how you work in HubSpot.** This system reads from HubSpot, thinks about what it sees, and delivers the insights to you. It also writes key findings back into HubSpot so your team benefits too.

## What It Looks Like Day to Day

### Morning: Daily Executive Brief
Every weekday at 7:00 AM, you receive an email with:
- **Pipeline health snapshot** — total pipeline, weighted pipeline, coverage ratio
- **Top 3 priorities** — what needs your attention today
- **Top 3 risks** — deals that are stalling, slipping, or going silent
- **Key opportunities** — deals with positive momentum
- **Rep signals** — who needs coaching, who's executing well
- **Recommended focus** — where your time creates the most leverage

This replaces the 30 minutes you currently spend digging through HubSpot every morning.

### During the Day: Proactive Alerts
You receive alerts only when something meaningful changes:
- **Deal Risk Alert** — a deal has gone silent, the close date slipped again, or a stage is stalled
- **Forecast Drift Alert** — your quarter forecast confidence dropped significantly
- **Rep Coaching Alert** — a rep's activity is declining or deals are being neglected
- **Opportunity Acceleration** — a big deal just gained momentum and you should lean in

These arrive via email and also create tasks in HubSpot so nothing falls through the cracks.

### Weekly: Forecast Memo
Every Monday morning, you get a structured forecast summary:
- Commit vs. best case vs. upside
- Coverage ratio and confidence score
- What moved in or out of the forecast this week
- Rep-level forecast breakdown
- Risks to the number

### Anytime: On-Demand Analysis
You can run specific analyses whenever you need them:
- **"Show me this deal"** — get a full intelligence summary on any deal
- **"Run a pipeline review"** — instant risk analysis across all open deals
- **"Give me coaching notes on this rep"** — scorecard, talking points, and action items
- **"Process these meeting notes"** — turn rough bullet points into clean summaries with action items

## The Six AI Agents

These agents are the thinking layer. They analyze your HubSpot data and produce actionable insights.

### 1. Pipeline Risk Agent
**Job:** Find deals that are about to stall, slip, or die — before you notice.

How it works:
- Checks every open deal for warning signs: no activity in 7+ days, close date pushed multiple times, stuck in the same stage for 14+ days, no next step defined, no emails or calls in 10+ days
- Scores each deal from 0 to 100 on health
- Flags deals as Healthy, At Risk, Critical, or Stalled
- Recommends specific actions: "Require a validated mutual close plan" or "Coach the rep on stage-specific exit criteria"

### 2. Forecast Confidence Agent
**Job:** Tell you how reliable your forecast actually is.

How it works:
- Calculates weighted pipeline, commit totals, coverage ratio
- Tracks stage velocity — how fast deals are moving (or not)
- Compares current trajectory against historical close rates
- Generates a confidence score from 0 to 100
- Detects forecast drift — if the number is moving in the wrong direction, you know immediately

### 3. Deal Intelligence Agent
**Job:** Turn messy deal data into a clean, leadership-ready summary.

How it works:
- Pulls in all deal activity: calls, emails, meetings, notes, task completion
- Identifies blockers, stakeholder gaps, and engagement patterns
- Calculates a momentum score
- Generates a one-paragraph deal summary you can read in 15 seconds
- Recommends whether leadership intervention is needed and what kind

### 4. Rep Performance Agent
**Job:** Highlight coaching needs with evidence, not guesswork.

How it works:
- Scores each rep on: activity level, pipeline quality, forecast reliability, follow-up consistency, and win rate
- Identifies patterns: "This rep has 4 stalled deals and hasn't logged a call in 8 days"
- Generates coaching flags and specific 1:1 talking points
- Creates action items for both the rep and the manager

### 5. Executive Focus Agent
**Job:** Synthesize everything into "what matters today."

How it works:
- Takes the output from all other agents
- Distills it into three lists:
  - **Top 3 priorities** — where your time creates the most impact today
  - **Top 3 risks** — what could hurt the quarter if ignored
  - **Top 3 leverage moves** — specific actions that accelerate revenue
- Generates a concise executive summary paragraph

### 6. Meeting Summary Agent
**Job:** Process your meetings so nothing gets lost.

How it works:
- You paste in rough notes, bullet points, or a transcript
- It outputs: clean summary, decisions made, action items with owners and due dates, deal updates, and follow-ups
- Works for: pipeline reviews, rep 1:1s, forecast calls, leadership meetings, and team meetings
- Action items get routed to the right place in the system

## How HubSpot Gets Smarter

This system doesn't just read from HubSpot — it writes intelligence back. Your entire team benefits from insights that are generated automatically.

### What Gets Written Back to HubSpot

**On every deal:**
| Property | What It Shows |
|----------|--------------|
| Deal Health Status | Healthy / At Risk / Critical / Stalled |
| Forecast Risk Level | Low / Medium / High / Critical |
| Days Since Last Activity | How long since meaningful engagement |
| Leadership Attention Needed | Yes / No flag for VP-level deals |
| Agent Recommended Action | "Schedule executive sponsor meeting" or "Validate mutual close plan" |
| Deal Summary Snapshot | One-line deal summary visible in list views |
| Close Confidence Score | 0-100 probability based on behavioral signals |

**On each rep (owner-level):**
| Property | What It Shows |
|----------|--------------|
| Activity Score | Overall activity rating |
| Follow-Up Consistency | Excellent / Good / Needs Improvement / Poor |
| Forecast Reliability | How accurate their forecasts have been |
| Stalled Deal Count | Number of deals not moving |
| Coaching Priority | Low / Medium / High / Urgent |

This means you can build **HubSpot dashboards and saved views** filtered by these properties. For example:
- A saved view showing all deals where `Leadership Attention Needed = Yes`
- A dashboard filtered to `Deal Health Status = Critical`
- A list of reps sorted by `Coaching Priority = High`

The system creates intelligence. HubSpot displays it. You act on it.

## How You Can Manage It

You have full control over this system. Here's what you can do:

### Use It Passively (Recommended Start)
- Just read the daily brief emails every morning
- Review alerts when they arrive
- Let the system write intelligence back to HubSpot
- Use HubSpot dashboards filtered by the new properties

You don't need to open Obsidian at all if you don't want to. The email briefs and HubSpot properties give you 80% of the value.

### Use It Actively
If you want deeper engagement:
- **Open the Obsidian vault** to browse deal notes, coaching records, and pipeline reviews
- **Add your own notes** — leadership observations, meeting takeaways, strategic thoughts
- **Use the daily note template** to log your priorities and decisions each day
- **Search across everything** — Obsidian's search finds any deal, rep, or topic instantly
- **Link notes together** — connect a coaching session to specific deals, or a forecast note to a pipeline review

### Customize It
- **Adjust alert thresholds** — change how many days of silence triggers a risk flag (default: 7 days)
- **Change notification schedules** — daily brief at 6 AM instead of 7 AM, weekly forecast on Friday instead of Monday
- **Modify templates** — add fields that matter to your specific process
- **Add new playbooks** — document your sales methodology, competitive responses, or objection handling in the Playbooks folder

### Add Team Members (Future)
The system can be expanded to:
- Give each sales manager their own daily brief for their team
- Create team-specific dashboards using the HubSpot custom properties
- Share the Obsidian vault (read-only or collaborative) with select leaders
- Route specific alerts to specific managers based on deal ownership

## How to Get Started

The system is already built. Activation takes about an hour:

1. **Connect HubSpot** — create a Private App in your HubSpot portal and add the access token
2. **Run first sync** — pulls all your deals into the system
3. **Set up email** — configure SMTP so daily briefs and alerts can be delivered
4. **Turn on the scheduler** — automated daily briefs, weekly forecasts, and pipeline syncs begin

After that, it runs on autopilot. You receive intelligence. You lead with clarity.
